## 🤖 What is Prime Spot Grid Trading Bot?

**Prime Spot Grid Trading Bot** is an enterprise-grade desktop application designed to automate your cryptocurrency trading on Binance. By utilizing a highly optimized Spot Grid and DCA (Dollar-Cost Averaging) engine, the bot continuously buys low and sells high within a dynamic price range, generating passive income in sideways or volatile markets.

Whether you are a beginner looking for "Set & Forget" preset strategies or a pro trader needing advanced risk management like Circuit Breakers and Trailing Down, Prime Grid Bot is built for you.

## ✨ Core Features (Why choose Prime?)

* 📈 **Dynamic Auto Range:** Automatically calculates the optimal grid floor and ceiling using **Bollinger Bands**, **ATR (Average True Range)**, or Recent High/Low.
* 🛡️ **DCA Safety Net:** Built-in Martingale rescue strategy. If the price dumps below your grid, the bot automatically averages down your entry price to exit the stuck position safely.
* 🚀 **Sliding Grid (Infinity Grid):** The bot automatically shifts the grid upward during a bull run, ensuring you never miss out on profits when a coin pumps out of range.
* 📉 **Trailing Down:** Follows the price down to lock in a new floor, maximizing capital efficiency.
* 🎮 **Paper Trading & Backtester:** Test your strategies on live Binance Mainnet data with fake money, or run historical backtests to simulate profitability before risking real capital.
* 🔐 **Absolute Security:** Your API keys are encrypted and stored locally on your machine. **We do not require and strictly prohibit withdrawal permissions.**
